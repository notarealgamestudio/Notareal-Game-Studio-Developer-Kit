Notareal Game Studio is an offline game-making studio. Pick a game type, change the settings, press Play, and export a ROM or a PC game. It comes with built-in testing, presets drawn from a library of measured real-game data, and no code is needed to make platformers, RPGs, fighting and wrestling games, minigames, puzzles, a music maker and more.
